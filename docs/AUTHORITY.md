# Authority

Authority flows downward from explicit doctrine; generated evidence never flows upward into expectations.

1. **Specification** in `spec/` defines the product and semantic doctrine. Research II supersedes contradictory Research I conclusions.
2. **Authored vectors** in `vectors/` are normative test cases when interpreted with their classification, evidence, expectation, context, and lifecycle.
3. **Registries** in `registry/` govern reusable source identity, semantic axes, versioned dialect identity, and permanent vector IDs.
4. **Reference matcher** in `reference/cron_ref.py` independently derives selected cron expectations from explicit policies. It is a scoring/reference artifact, not a production engine and not Rust runtime code.
5. **Observations** are measurements produced by external engines under named builds, profiles, dialects, runtimes, and tzdb provenance. They are never normative.
6. **Golden fixtures** are test fixtures for serializer and scorer behavior. They prove tooling stability, not recurrence semantics.
7. **Generated reports and packs** are reproducible distributions of authored data. They may be deleted and rebuilt; they do not supersede source JSON.
8. **Historical evidence** under `legacy/phase2-rc1/` preserves Research II RC1 vectors, adapters, raw observations, matrices, and tools for audit. Its incumbent observations and overloaded error status must never define future expectations.

Compatibility claims are meaningful only with a named corpus version, dialect/profile, policy, engine build, runtime, and timezone database provenance. A bare claim such as “cron-compatible” is insufficient.
