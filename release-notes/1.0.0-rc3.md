## Authority corrections

### CRON-DST-020

The rc2 construction used `30 2 * * SUN`, started on 2026-03-01, and requested
four results. It stopped on March 22, before London's March 29 transition; even
on transition Sunday, 02:30 is valid because London skips 01:00–01:59.

The rc3 construction uses `30 1 * * SUN` and requests five results. It reaches
the nonexistent 2026-03-29 01:30 wall time. Skip continues to April 5,
gap-start/next-valid resolve to 01:00Z (02:00 BST), and pre-gap-offset resolves
to 01:30Z (02:30 BST). Expectations changed.

### CRON-DST-001

The rc2 `fire_at_gap_start` note cited RFC 5545's pre-gap-offset rule while its
answer clamped to New York's 07:00Z gap boundary. Rc3 preserves that clamp under
`fire_at_gap_start` and adds the mechanically distinct `pre_gap_offset` case:
nominal 02:30 interpreted with UTC-05:00 is 07:30Z, normalized to 03:30
UTC-04:00. Expectations changed by adding this answer; the existing clamp
answer remains unchanged.

### CRON-DST-015

The rc2 rationale described a historical Newfoundland transition near
00:01–01:00. IANA tzdb 2026a advances St_Johns from 01:59:59 UTC-03:30 to
03:00:00 UTC-02:30. The authored 23:30 occurrences were already correct and are
unchanged. Rc3 describes the vector as a fractional-offset control outside the
gap and fold.

## Evidence and compatibility

The transition arithmetic was independently reproduced against the pinned IANA
tzdb 2026a certification data and IANA tzdb 2026c; the three relevant 2026
transitions are identical in both releases. The new `tzdb-2026a` source registry
entry points to IANA's primary release distribution. RFC 5545 §3.3.5 and verified
erratum 4271 are attached only to `pre_gap_offset`; crontab(5) supports only the
daemon skip case.

`correction-report.json` enumerates the exact semantic and metadata change
surface. `migration-report.json` and `legacy/phase2-rc1/` remain immutable RC1→RC2
history and were not regenerated.

At initial publication of these correction notes, tooling `0.1.0-rc2` remained
locked to corpus rc2. Tooling `0.1.0-rc3` now consumes this corrected authority,
but no RC3 certification evidence has yet been produced.

Protocol 3.0 was subsequently added during that tooling/provenance hardening.
This protocol-only package change does not alter any vector, expectation, corpus
version, or canonical vector digest. Protocol 2.0 remains available only to
interpret historical RC2 evidence.
